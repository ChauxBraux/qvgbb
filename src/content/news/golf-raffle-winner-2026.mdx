We're thrilled to announce that **Samuel Thomas** is the winner of our 2026 Father's Day Golf Raffle. Congratulations, Samuel — enjoy the foursomes, the new clubs, and a very well-earned Father's Day.

## Thank you for showing up

Every ticket sold goes directly to the program — gear, tournament fees, team meals, and the things that make a season feel like a season for these girls. We're grateful to everyone who bought a chance, shared the raffle with friends and family, or forwarded it along to a golfer in their life.

A special thank you to **Shannopin Country Club** and **Beaver Valley Golf Club** for donating the foursomes that made this prize package possible.

## What's next

We'll be back next year with another raffle, and there are always ways to support the program in the meantime — through [Venmo](/donate) or by becoming a [corporate sponsor](/sponsors) for the 2026-2027 season.

Thank you, all of you.
